Grain and sources

  • Fact grain: one row per scheduled service visit (fact_service_visit).
  • Dashboard source: agg_daily_branch_service, one row per day, branch and service, rebuilt from the fact table.
  • Comparisons: change is measured against the same dates one year earlier, so seasonal swings do not look like growth or decline.
  • Customer plan: stored as a slowly changing dimension (type 2), so each visit points at the plan the customer had on the visit date.
